How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain spread × segment. Every figure comes from the snapshot itself; no protocol name is ever special-cased.

  1. Category multiple. The median market-cap / TVL ratio of that category's traded protocols in this snapshot, each ratio clamped to [0.02, 5], then shrunk toward the whole market's median (weight 4). A category with no traded member takes the market median. Market median: —.
  2. Growth. 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) × 0.6. A hot week is worth 60% of its face; no reported change scores neutral.
  3. Maturity. 0.65 + 0.35 × min(tracked days, 1460) / 1460, times 0.85 when tracked under 90 days. An unrecorded listing date scores the snapshot's median age (— days).
  4. Chain spread. 1 + 0.015 per extra chain, up to eight.
  5. Segment. Launch candidates score 1.00. Products of a traded token and exchanges score 0.30 — a product of a token that already trades cannot launch a token of its own at the full comparable value, and an exchange token would share value with the exchange itself.
